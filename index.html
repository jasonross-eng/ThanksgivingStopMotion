🎨 How to Make a Stop Motion Animation!

  1. 1 Pick festive stickers (like Pilgrims, turkeys & foxes) and add words to your first slide. Tap a Pilgrim to change their skin color!
  2. 2 Click "📸 Next Slide (Copy & Move)". It copies your picture!
  3. 3 Move or turn your characters a little bit on the new slide. Follow the 👻 Ghost Helper!
  4. 4 Tap "🔊 Add Sound" for gobbles and music, then hit "▶️ Play Stop Motion Animation!"
  5. 5 Made a mistake? Tap "↩️ Undo". Finished? Tap "💾 Save & Share" to get your project code.
