🎬 How to Make a Stop Motion Movie!

  1. 1 Pick festive stickers (like turkeys & pies) and add words to your first slide.
  2. 2 Click "📸 Next Slide (Copy & Move)". It copies your picture!
  3. 3 Move or turn your turkey a little bit on the new slide. Follow the 👻 Ghost Helper!
  4. 4 Tap "🔊 Add Sound" for gobbles and music, then hit "🎬 Create Stop Motion Movie!"
  5. 5 Made a mistake? Tap "↩️ Undo". Finished? Tap "💾 Save & Share" to get your movie code.
